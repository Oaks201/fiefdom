# Crossing companies — review concepts

Generated with the built-in image editor from the Fiefdom's approved company portraits. Every prompt called for a transparent shoulder-up bust, bold dark outlines, broad hand-painted Warcraftian shapes, and the player's green-and-silver colors. The paths follow the game codex's six crossing names and stage order.

| Crossing | Stage I prompt | Stage II prompt | Stage III prompt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barracks × Foundry | Fair, freckled shield-bearing Ironclad in practical forged steel | Same veteran as an armored Cataphract with one barded horse | Same mounted veteran as the heavier Iron Legion with a green crest |
| Barracks × Mage Tower | Agile Spellblade with only a sheathed sword's hilt visible behind her shoulder | Same swordswoman in stronger warded plate, with a brighter hilt glow | Same knight under a green hood and arcane face veil, with only the hilt visible |
| Barracks × Merchant Hall | Paid Beastcatcher with rope and snare | Same hunter with recurved bow and quiver | Same hunter with fur-edged hood, antler pauldron and trophy fang |
| Foundry × Mage Tower | Simple terracotta Clay Golem with quiet violet core | Approved Rune Golem design faithfully redrawn with smoother edges | Same block-built form as a larger Runeforged Titan with brighter chest core |
| Foundry × Merchant Hall | Dwarven War Wagon crew with a small mounted bolt launcher | Same crew with a straight wheeled bombard | Same crew with a dragon-shaped muzzle shroud and contained firelight |
| Mage Tower × Merchant Hall | Deep-brown-skinned Alchemist with violet flask and practical merchant coat | Same woman with a one-eye gilt piece and mirror echo | Same covert agent hooded, gold-masked, with a stronger violet echo |

These are review-only assets; no company art has been installed in the game. Each stage's folder contains full portraits (512 × 512), tokens (128 × 128), 48-pixel icons, its source images, and a style comparison against approved units. The prior Rune Golem source remains byte-identical to `src/renderer/public/game-assets/company.runeGolems.portrait-early-v3.png`; the new preview redraw is separate.

The Spellblade line retains its previous appearance. The Ironclad line now has fair, freckled features; the Alchemist line has deep-brown skin and dark curls. Each woman's appearance remains consistent across stages, with equipment and progression preserved. The Rune Golem's pixel staircases were traced into clean hand-painted contours, using the adjacent Clay Golem and Runeforged Titan as style references while preserving its block layout, violet gems, and gold bands.
