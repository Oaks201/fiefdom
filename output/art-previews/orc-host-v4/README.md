# Orc host clan colors — review pass

Open `index.html` to compare this pass with `orc-host-v3`; `contact-sheet.png` shows all five beside the approved Orc Warlord. The concepts are staged for review and have not been installed in the game.

The Grunt and Wolf Rider were brought toward a shared mid-olive clan green. The Shaman and Brute already sat close to that palette, so their portraits were preserved. Ugrak's Warboss was brought closer to the same green, and the metal bands at the bases of both lower tusks were redrawn to follow the teeth cleanly. His manticore head-pelt and heavy armor remain.

`sources/` retains the full transparent source images. `prepare.cjs` exports 512 px portraits, 128 px tokens and 48 px icon previews.
