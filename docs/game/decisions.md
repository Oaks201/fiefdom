# Decisions, assumptions and errata

This file settles what the design book ([design-book-v2.md](design-book-v2.md)) leaves open, contradicts, or gets wrong. **Where the two disagree, this file wins.**

- **D-** entries were decided by the owner. Do not change them.
- **A-** entries are planning assumptions: sensible defaults chosen so work can start. The owner may override any of them. To override one, edit the line, add `(changed YYYY-MM-DD)`, and tell the agents working on affected tasks.
- **E-** entries correct worked examples and tests in the book. Tests use the E- values.
- Numbers marked **TUNE** are first guesses. They live in `rules.ts` like every other number, and the simulator reports on them.

**For agents:** if you hit an ambiguity that is not listed here, do not invent a rule silently. Pick the most conservative reading, add an entry under [Raised by agents](#raised-by-agents) at the bottom, and mention it in your hand-off.

## A — Planning assumptions (owner may override)

### Time and data

| ID | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| A-01 | **A campaign day is a ledger calendar date.** Day D uses the ledger's date D (steps, food, duties, weight), because the ledger and Fitbit both bucket by calendar date. Day D *closes* at 04:00 on D+1 in the campaign time zone. The book's "04:00 to 04:00" describes when a day settles, not how data is bucketed. |
| A-02 | **Corrections.** Day D can be corrected until day D+1 closes (04:00 on D+2). Settlement copies the inputs it used into the campaign event log. A correction inside the grace window posts `adjust` events; it can raise a contract payout but never lower one already paid (book Ch 2 rule 5). Ledger edits after the window never change the game. |
| A-03 | **Time zone.** The campaign time zone is the system IANA zone at founding (`Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone`), stored in `Campaign.timeZone`. Settlement always uses the stored zone. Changing it is a settings action that takes effect at the next week boundary. All clock functions must work for any IANA zone, whatever the machine's zone. |
| A-04 | **Founding and week 1.** The campaign starts at the dawn after founding. Campaign week 1 runs from that dawn to the next week close (04:00 on `settings.weekStartsOn`) and may be partial. Weekly amounts (step pool, weekly reputation, the rivals' income) are prorated by days ÷ 7 in a partial first week. "Campaign week N" = 1 + the number of week closes since the start. Pacing rules ("week 36", "week 12") use this number. |
| A-05 | **Weigh-ins live in the ledger** as `DayLog.weight` (hand-typed, in `settings.unit`). The ledger version goes from 1 to 2, and `normalizeLedger` migrates v1 by copying each legacy contract's `startWeight` and `finalWeight` onto its start and close dates (only where that day has no weight). Weight rules compute in lb; kg values are converted. |
| A-06 | **Total calories burned for the Healer.** The Healer's bootstrap (Ch 4, step 2) needs total daily calories burned, including resting. The current Fitbit code reads only active calories. Add a read-only `burned` metric from Google Health's `total-calories`. It is used only to bootstrap the Healer's range, never for scoring, and is never hand-editable. |
| A-07 | **The legacy ledger system.** A campaign can be founded only when no legacy contract is open, upcoming or awaiting a weigh-in. After founding, the Contract page seals only campaign contracts. Legacy contracts and legacy (computed) reputation stay readable in the Archive as history and never enter the purse. Once a campaign exists, the top bar shows the campaign purse. |
| A-08 | **File locations.** The book's `data/codex/*.json` → `src/renderer/src/data/codex/*.json`. The book's `data/text/*.json` → `src/renderer/src/data/text/*.json`. The book's `assets/` and `assets/manifest.json` → `src/renderer/public/game-assets/` and `src/renderer/public/game-assets/manifest.json` (Vite already writes its own build output to `assets/`). `campaign.json` sits beside `ledger.json` in the data folder, with backups at `backups/campaign-YYYY-MM-DD.json`. |
| A-09 | **Dev-only time travel.** Development builds may override "now" (env `FIEFDOM_DEV_NOW`, plus a dev menu that advances a day or a week) so settlement can be tested. It is guarded by `import.meta.env.DEV`, compiled out of production builds, and never reachable by the player (Pillar 7). |
| A-10 | **"Away"** (for the rule that no siege falls within 7 days of returning) means no app launch for 14 or more days. `campaign.json` records the last launch date. |
| A-11 | **Rounding.** Each purse event is rounded to 0.1 when it is posted. The purse is displayed as a whole number, rounded down. Affordability checks use the exact balance. |

### Combat and rivals

| ID | Assumption |
| --- | --- |
| A-26 | **Choosing the raider.** On a raid day the raider is drawn from rivals in Tension or War (not resolved, allied, or under a Truce or pact that blocks raids). Each rival's weight is its raid-frequency multiplier × (1 + number of player border hexes it touches) × 0.5 if it is at war with another rival that week × 1.25 if it is in a coalition. If no rival is eligible, the day's threat becomes beasts. The 40/20/40 mix is the share of days; personality multipliers decide *who* raids, not how often raids happen. |
| A-27 | **Targets without a shared border.** A raid from a rival that shares no border with the player hits the player border hex nearest (by hex distance) to that rival's land; ties are broken by a seeded draw. Mythics prefer border hexes in the West and East between-lands (weighted ring^1.5) and fall back to any border hex. |
| A-28 | **Threat schedule timing.** Each threat's *type* and raider are drawn at week close for the coming week. Its *target* and its ±15% roll are drawn at that day's dawn, on the border as it stands then. |
| A-29 | **Enemies in Grand Battles** deal `p × intent multiplier` with no tag matching and an implied Readiness of 1.0. Only the player's companies get the ×1.5 / ×0.6 match. |
| A-30 | "Wonder bonuses" (Ch 10 and Ch 11) are left over from version 1. Read them as Milestone bonuses: the Proving Grounds, the Crownguard Ascendant, and Legendary and trophy items. |
| A-31 | **Stacking.** Each building-tier effect in the Ch 7 table is that tier's total, not cumulative (Foundry III = walls +6 and all companies +10%). Percentage *reductions* from different sources multiply. Percentage *reputation bonuses* add (Merchant Hall +15% + Statue +10% + Gilded Ledger +5% = +30%). Guild Charters' 20% replaces Trade Roads' 10%; they do not stack. |
| A-32 | **Courtship slots.** Base 2; +1 at Merchant Hall III (Ch 6); +1 more at Merchant Hall V (Ch 7); +1 for the Golden Road Wing. Chapters 6 and 7 disagree, so both are applied. |
| A-33 | **Incursion frequency.** An Incursion triggers when the player takes a hex next to that rival's Gate, or takes one of its hexes while already holding 4 or more hexes that rival once held. At most one Incursion per rival every 14 days (TUNE). The 5-day Grand Battle spacing still applies. |
| A-34 | **"The player's strongest direction"** (Rising Crown coalition) is the between-land (North, South, West or East) where the player holds the most Dominion. Its two rivals are the two whose roads bound it: North = Orc and Goblin, South = Archmage and Dwarf, West = Orc and Archmage, East = Goblin and Dwarf. |
| A-35 | **Host filling.** The commander joins first if the power budget covers it. Then the builder makes repeated passes over the rival's unit list, strongest to weakest, adding one company of each type that still fits. It stops when nothing fits or the host has 6 companies. |
| A-36 | **Orders don't carry over.** With no orders, every company defends (Ch 2 rule 2). The UI may offer a one-click "repeat yesterday's orders". |
| A-37 | **Rim fronts at peace.** Each week a front spends at Peace moves its war track one step back toward 0 (version 1 rule). |
| A-38 | **Prized habits** (+4 Respect a week): Orc — a week with no lost battle of any kind; Goblin — the week's budget score is 100% with food logged on at least 5 days (TUNE); Archmage — every sworn duty kept every day; Dwarf — the step pool met (S_w = 1). |
| A-47 | **"Health ×N" on a mythic** (Basilisk ×6, Dragon ×8, Manticore ×5) means health = N × p, replacing the usual 4 × p, not 4 × p × N. |

The same holds for every unlisted effect (Barracks I and II keep the 50% rally floor; features such as hired blades or foresight stay from their tier up). T01, 2026-10-06.
- **A-104** — The Royal Hunt (Legend perk) pays 60 reputation "and a trophy item", but every Appendix C trophy comes from a mythic. Reading until the owner names one: the hunt pays the 60 reputation; the codex marks `trophy: true` and grants no particular item. T01, 2026-10-06.

Every legacy contract still in the ledger counts, burned wagers included (their start weight was a real weigh-in). A weight in the other unit is converted to `settings.unit` and rounded to 0.1. T02, 2026-10-06.
- **A-107** — Burning a legacy contract erases the steps and calories on its days. It now keeps that day's weigh-in and Fitbit's total calories burned, because they are health records rather than the contract's progress. T02, 2026-10-06.
- **A-108** — A-13's village rules can't all hold. Fully apart, 18 seeded villages split 4/6/8 don't fit on this map even ignoring the rival villages (an exhaustive search finds none). With the rival villages untouched, ring 4 has room for exactly 8 villages, and those leave ring 3 room for only 4. Reading kept until the owner decides: the counts stay (30 villages, 4/6/8), no seeded village touches a rival village or a village in its own ring, and at most **2** pairs of seeded villages touch across neighboring rings (the fewest that fits). That leaves 41 possible layouts, all within the credit spread of 1. `RULES.map.villageSeeding.maxTouchingPairs` holds the 2. The alternative is fewer villages: fully apart, 4/4/8 (28 villages) fits. T03, 2026-10-06.
- **A-109** — `hexLabel` numbers each ring from **1** at its NW corner, clockwise (the castle is "0-1"), since the labels are for players to read ("hex 3-4"). T03, 2026-10-06.
- **A-110** — How `claimableBy` reads the land rules. `buy` is the player buying an adjacent rival hex (never neutral land, never a Gate). `rivalExpand` (A-25) targets adjacent neutral hexes only. A rival may `assault` (a conquest attempt) but never `court` a player's village (A-23). A Lair Mouth or capital is false for every method because Grand Battles don't go through `claimableBy`. A Gate can be courted. Contested and scorched status, costs, slots and Respect are left to the systems that use it. T03, 2026-10-06.
- **A-111** — Ch 4 scores "each calendar week the contract touches", but a contract can end mid-week, before its week does. Each week's three pillars use only the contract's own days in that week: steps against the pool's share for those days, the Table over those days, duties over those days. T04, 2026-10-06.
- **A-112** — The book's "Merchant Hall bonus on every gain above" includes the contract payout and pledge return. The reputation bonus applies to the payout (and a withdrawal's payout) but not to the pledge's return, which carries the player's own stake; otherwise a pledge would break even below 70%. T04, 2026-10-06.
- **A-113** — Respite (Ch 4 rule 4) is a contract rule. It needs a running contract that covers the day, and it removes the day only from that contract's score. Weekly reputation, Valor and Realm Consistency still count the day. T04, 2026-10-06.
- **A-114** — Withdrawal's `daysElapsed` counts the contract's days from its start through the withdrawal day, inclusive, less Respite days (the T04 test pays 4 days on day 4). A queued contract then starts at the next dawn. Sealing re-checks the Charter against the current Healer floor, so a floor that has risen above the limit blocks the next seal until the limit is revised (Ch 4 Healer rule 5), unless medical supervision is confirmed. T04, 2026-10-06.
- **A-115** — Ch 9 Lock 1 says the mark is reached when "the 7-day average is" at or below it, but a lone weigh-in in its window is its own average, which would skip the two-weigh-in rule. The average route needs at least 2 weigh-ins in the 7-day window. Reaching a mark is remembered (any day up to the week close counts), since "a Milestone reached early waits for its week". T05, 2026-10-06.
- **A-116** — Keeping Milestones (Ch 9 rule 2) don't say their mark, earliest week or how runs count. Their mark is the goal and their earliest week is the goal's. They break in order. Each needs its own run of 4 straight week closes with the 7-day average within 2% of the goal, counted only after the previous Milestone broke. The Dispensation does not apply to them, because they are about holding weight, not a stalled loss. T05, 2026-10-06.
- **A-117** — The Healer's Dispensation: "8 straight weeks" counts week closes in a row with Realm Consistency at 0.85 or more, this one included. "Whose earliest week has passed" means the current week is past it (strictly later). "At most one per 8 weeks" counts from the week of the last Dispensation break. It breaks only the next unbroken Milestone, at most one per week close, and a too-fast week pauses it like any other break (A-43). T05, 2026-10-06.
- **A-118** — Goal changes (Ch 9 rule 8). The remaining Milestones are replanned from the last broken mark (or the start) to the new goal with the same step rules over the remaining count. Earliest weeks still count from the start weight (E-04). The 28 days count from the last change: the founding goal is not a change, so a mistyped goal can be fixed at once. A new goal at or above the last broken mark makes every remaining Milestone a Keeping Milestone. T05, 2026-10-06.
- **A-119** — Momentum (Ch 5). "Current weight" is the 7-day average, or the latest weigh-in when the week has none. Maintenance (rule 7) needs the 7-day average within 2% of the goal both ways (2% of the average and 2% of the goal). The too-fast hold (rule 6) wins over maintenance's Mw = 1, since it is the safety rule. T05, 2026-10-06.
- **A-120** — Ch 16 "Illness and travel" has the Steward point to Respite "when a rough patch shows", without saying what a rough patch is. It is 3 low-scoring days in a row (TUNE, `RULES.healer.roughPatchDays`). Whoever calls `healerCheckIns` (T06) decides what a low-scoring day is and passes the count. T05, 2026-10-06.
- **A-121** — A-02's grace window, as settlement checks it. Only the last settled day can still be corrected. `settle` takes a correction to it while the open day is at most 2 days later, so the call made at (or the launch right after) the next close still picks up an edit typed just before 04:00. A launch later than that ignores the edit. A correction re-scores daily and weekly income and contracts, and posts an `adjust` for any increase only. A decrease updates the snapshot, so a still-running contract scores lower from then on, but nothing already paid is lowered. T06, 2026-10-07.
- **A-122** — The Charter stores duties by name, and the ledger marks habits done by id. A sworn duty counts as kept when the ledger habit with that id is done, or else the habit with that name (trimmed, ignoring case). A day's duties are judged by the Charter of the contract covering it, or else the campaign's Charter. T06, 2026-10-07.
- **A-123** — A timer's date (`statusUntil`, `wearyUntil`, `settlingUntil`, a deal's `until`) is the last day the status holds. It expires at that day's close. The `expireTimers` phase removes expired Truces, pacts and calls to arms from `deals`, and keeps hex deals as history. T06, 2026-10-07.
- **A-124** — Which terms judge what. Weekly income and Realm Consistency use the Charter governing the week's last day with the current (smoothed) Healer floor. A contract is scored by its own sealed Charter and the floor sealed with it. T06, 2026-10-07.
- **A-125** — At founding, the Healer floor is computed from the ledger's last 28 days (logs, else Fitbit burned, else Mifflin–St Jeor, else 1,200) and set directly, since A-44's first computation sets the floor. Those 28 days are snapshotted, so the trend and Healer windows can look back from week 1, and later ledger edits to them change nothing. T06, 2026-10-07.
- **A-126** — Stacking beyond A-31. Percentage *gains* from different sources add, like reputation bonuses: company power shares (the Proving Grounds +10%, Engine Works +10%, a trophy's ×1.15 counts as +15%), tithes (Guild Charters +50% and the Counting House +25% make ×1.75). Reductions multiply, Weary's −20% included. "Walls count double" from Shieldwall and Runed Walls never stacks: a ring-5 mythic attack counts the walls twice, not four times. Foretelling days from different sources add (Mage Tower IV plus the Spy Network warn of a raid 2 days ahead). T07, 2026-10-07.
- **A-127** — Trade Roads and Guild Charters ("tiers and Crossings cost less") discount building tiers and Crossing stages, not castle tiers. T07, 2026-10-07.
- **A-128** — A Legendary item's realm-wide effect (the Unbroken Banner, the Gilded Ledger, the Starglass Orb, the Anvil-Heart) holds while the item is equipped on any company, not while it sits unequipped in the Armory. T07, 2026-10-07.

T08, 2026-10-07.
- **A-134** — The Royal Hunt: "one beast attack a week becomes a hunt" is read as the first beast attack of the week that the player *beats*. It pays the hunt's 60 reputation (with the reputation bonus) in place of its spoils, plus A-131's trophy event. A lost beast attack is an ordinary loss and doesn't use the hunt. T08, 2026-10-07.
- **A-135** — Grand Illusion: "one lost battle a week costs no tribute and does not contest a hex". On a lost daily threat it waives the tribute, and the hex is still scorched (scorching isn't named). On a lost first strike of a conquest attempt the hex isn't contested and the attempt ends. It doesn't apply to a restrike on a hex already contested. "A week" is the calendar week; uses are counted from the log. T08, 2026-10-07.
- **A-136** — Timers and land changing hands in combat. A lost daily threat scorches the hex for the 3 days after the battle. A conquest attempt that wins on day D contests the hex through D + 1 (D + 2 at Grace II); the same force strikes on each contested day, and a loss on the last one passes the hex at the next dawn. A hex that changes hands loses its fortification (it belonged to the old owner); a hex lost to a rival gets that rival's garrison (A-17) and, for a village, rival loyalty 30 × ring. Bounties double spoils for taking a beast den too ("victories over beasts"). T08, 2026-10-07.
- **A-137** — Hired blades and rival envoys (Ch 5 sinks) join only the day's defense pool. Each battle that fields one costs its fee (20, or 25 for an envoy) when it is fought, and one the purse can't pay for stays out of that battle. Emboldened and Humbled raids read the rival's current front tracks (+3 / −3) or `humbledUntil`; T10 decides when those change. T08, 2026-10-07.
- **A-138** — The Ch 10 worked battle (E-02) fields the Battlemages, a Barracks + Mage Tower Brotherhood hybrid, but uses a 55% rally floor. That Brotherhood also brings Warded Steel (+5%), so the same realm actually fights at 60%: 117.9 × (0.60 + 0.40 × 0.97) = 116.49 on the full day. Test 3 checks E-02's numbers with r = 0.55 given directly, and the realm-state test checks the 60%. Nothing is changed; noted so nobody "fixes" one to match the other. T08, 2026-10-07.
- **A-139** — Reclaiming (Ch 6, Grace I). "Lost in the last 14 days" is read from the log: the last `hexTransfer` away from the player, at most 14 days before today (10 days works, 15 doesn't). It works whoever holds the hex now, if it still touches the player's land. The price is half its garrison *strength*, as the Ch 6 garrison table counts it: garrison plus 25% of base per fortification level, without this week's assault wear. The hex comes back like a conquered one (A-22: half loyalty, Settling for 4 weeks), since the book doesn't say, and Respect doesn't change. T09, 2026-10-07.
- **A-140** — A bought or sold hex changes hands when the deal is struck, not at the next dawn. Deals are made on the open day and there is no settlement phase for pending transfers; the day's tiding was fixed at dawn, and combat already redraws a tiding whose target is no longer the player's. T09, 2026-10-07.
- **A-141** — Courtships, where Ch 6 is silent. With two suitors the higher Offer wins the contest (a tie goes to the player; among rivals, the usual order Orc, Goblin, Dwarf, Archmage), and the winner must still reach the resistance. Whoever ends up with the village has spent the whole bid; every other bidder, player or rival, gets half back (a rival's half goes to its treasury). A village that holds loses 10% of the highest Offer. Offers and resistance are compared at the purse's 0.1 precision (A-11), so 50 × 0.9 meets 45. The owner's counter-bid is never refunded. T10 takes rival bids from the treasury when it places them (`placeRivalBid`). Envoy's Rest's +0.05 is added after the clamp, so Trust can reach 1.25. A courtship whose village is no longer courtable at the close (taken another way, or no longer touching the player's land) is void and refunded in full. T09, 2026-10-07.
- **A-142** — Deals, where Ch 6 is silent. A Truce is refused while any Grand Battle is announced and not yet fought, not only one against that rival. Its "highest ring that rival borders" is the highest ring of the rival's hexes that touch the player's land, or of all its land when none does. Only trades, Truces (+2) and sales (+5) change Respect; pacts and calls to arms don't. A second Truce, pact or call to arms can't be bought while one is in force. A Gate is never sold either. The sale price uses the undiscounted buy price: the Exchange Wing discounts only what the player pays. A call to arms needs a Rim front between the two rivals (non-neighbors have nothing to fight over) and is refused if they share a coalition. The 4-week limit counts buys and sells together, from the last one. T09, 2026-10-07.
- **A-143** — The defection tally (Ch 14) counts a rival's original villages (its Gate and two March hexes at the founding) that the player holds now, each by the last way it reached the player other than a reclaim (influence, trade or conquest). Villages that came another way (an event, an abdication) aren't counted. `holds` counts every village the rival holds now, original or not. T13 decides whether "holds none" means only its original villages. T09, 2026-10-07.
- **A-144** — Rim fronts, where Ch 12 is silent. At each week close a front is drawn for the coming week: War at its war chance (40% touching the Orc, 25% otherwise), otherwise Peace or Tension with equal odds (TUNE, `RULES.rivalAi.frontPeaceShareOfRest`). A call to arms in force holds it at War; coalition partners' front is never at War (it reads Tension); a front with a resolved rival is at Peace. The draw comes at the end of the `borderCampaigns` phase, so a Border Campaign reads the state of the week it closes. T10, 2026-10-07.
- **A-145** — The Dwarf's fourth fortification level costs 120 × ring (TUNE, `RULES.rivalAi.dwarfLevel4CostPerRing`; the book gives only levels 1 to 3), less the Deep Halls' 25%. T10, 2026-10-07.
- **A-146** — Conquest attempts (Ch 12 step 7). "May declare one per day" is an upper bound; each rival at War plans one attempt a week (TUNE, `RULES.rivalAi.conquestPlansPerWeek`) at its week close, on the player's hex in ring 3 or beyond touching its land with the weakest expected defense, if 0.5 × AV ≥ 0.8 × that defense. The expected defense is the Marshal's best with every company defending, at the week's average Valor. It strikes on a seeded day 2 to 7 days after the close and is announced the day before (so the player sees it a day ahead). T08's `planConquest` checks week 6, Truces and pacts. T10, 2026-10-07.
- **A-147** — The Goblin's Market (Ch 12 step 6) spends a fund fed by its special share. First, mercenaries for each active rival at War with the player that has none (the Goblin included): +15% AV for 2 weeks, costing the power they add at that rival's army price (TUNE, `RULES.rivalAi.mercenaryCostShare`). Then, at most once every 4 weeks (TUNE, `RULES.rivalAi.marketEveryWeeks`), one hex bought from another active rival not at War with it: a hex touching the Goblin's land, never a Gate, capital, Lair Mouth, mythic hex or ring 0 to 2 hex, best Dominion per price, at the seller's trade price (50 × ring × greed, ×1.5 for a village), paid into the seller's treasury. Its counter-bids come from its reserve (step 8), not the fund. The mercenary bonus counts wherever the rival's AV does (raids, conquest attempts, skirmishes, Border Campaigns, Power, bands), but not in its own army prices. T10, 2026-10-07.
- **A-148** — The Archmage's Rituals. Its special share builds a fund. It casts the first Ritual no earlier than week 6 (TUNE), then one every 6 weeks once 400 is saved, in the codex's order and round again after the fourth, and only while it would strike the player (not at Peace, no Truce, pact or Accord). The Long Night and the Veil of Fog hold for the 7 days after the close. The Veil of Fog hides each tiding's band and exact strength, not the threat itself (combat's new `bandsHidden` hook; `tidingsHidden` stays for T13). The Summoning places its mythic garrison on a seeded neutral non-village hex touching the player's land. The Curse falls on the player's strongest stored company by current power. T10, 2026-10-07.
- **A-149** — Hostile acts and land taken (Ch 12 disposition and Threat). "Took its land" counts hexes the player took from that rival by conquest or by courting them away; trades and reclaims don't. A hostile act, for Peace's 4 quiet weeks, is one of its raids or conquest attempts striking (won or lost), the player's assault on its land, or a hex passing between them by conquest or influence. Windows are counted in days ending at the close (2 weeks = the last 14 days). T10, 2026-10-07.
- **A-150** — The Dwarf's 10% special (the Deep Halls) has no purchase of its own; it is spent on more fortification at the Deep Halls' 25% discount, with its fortify share. T10, 2026-10-07.
- **A-151** — The Orc's Warhost goes out at 600 saved, aimed at the player's border hex in rings 1 to 5 nearest the Orc's land (ties drawn), as a `WarhostRequest` for T12 (settlement collects them in `ctx.hooks.warhosts`), and the fund resets. It waits while the Orc is at Peace with the player or held off by a Truce, pact or Accord, and while the player has no border hex in rings 1 to 5. T10, 2026-10-07.
- **A-152** — Rival bids and the week close. A rival's expansion bid (A-25) leaves its treasury at its turn and waits for the next week close, so the player's bids placed during the week meet it there (Ch 6 "two suitors"). Counter-bids (step 8) are placed at that close too, just before courtships resolve: the player's sealed bids made during the week only exist then, and the treasury hasn't changed since the turn. A rival can't see a sealed bid, so it counter-bids its whole allowance: 60% of its reserve (the Goblin 100%), the reserve being its treasury up to 100, split evenly between its villages the player courts. Rival bids that meet no player bid resolve by A-141's rules (`resolveRivalCourtships`). T10, 2026-10-07.
- **A-153** — Skirmishes. A front's daily skirmishes are settled at the week close, one per day of the week, each drawn on its own day and won with the rival's share of the two AVs. The track (and with it Emboldened and Humbled) therefore changes once a week and acts on the next week's raids, as Ch 12 says. The week's 5% war loss comes after the skirmishes. AV losses shrink every company's power by the same share. T10, 2026-10-07.
- **A-154** — A rival's special fund (the Warhost, the Market, the Rituals) is unspent reputation, so its Power and its treasury band count it with the treasury. T10, 2026-10-07.
- **A-155** — The rival turn's open choices. Army: whole companies, strongest affordable first, each priced at the AV before it joins. Expansion: village ties go to the higher ring; a den is a neutral hex that is neither a village nor mythic, and "most valuable" means the highest ring, then the weakest garrison (fortification and wear counted, as in every garrison test here), then map order. Fortification: one level at a time on hexes touching land the rival doesn't hold, facing the player first, then the least fortified, then the innermost ring. Border Campaign targets: the touching hex with the weakest garrison; otherwise the defender's hex nearest the front's battlefields, ties drawn. T10, 2026-10-07.
