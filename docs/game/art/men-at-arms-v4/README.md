# Men-at-Arms portrait and token

Approved on 2026-10-10. The Tier II Men-at-Arms now grows visually from the revised Militia: blond hair and blue eyes, a full beard, broad silver shoulders, green cloth, and a sheathed sword behind the left shoulder. The rounded helmet and chainmail coif retain the old portrait's face-framing mail.

The [approved source](portrait-source.png) was generated with the built-in image generator as an edit of the [first draft](../../../../output/art-previews/men-at-arms-v2/portrait-source.png), with the [previous portrait](../superseded/men-at-arms-before-v4/company.menAtArms.portrait-early-v3.png) as the chainmail reference. The [prompt record](../../../../output/art-previews/men-at-arms-v2/prompt.md) and [comparison gallery](../../../../output/art-previews/men-at-arms-v2/index.html) preserve the iteration.

The installed files are `company.menAtArms.portrait-revised-v4.png` (512 × 512) and `company.menAtArms.token-revised-v4.png` (128 × 128) in `src/renderer/public/game-assets/`. Both come from a 256 × 256 contained logical image with nearest-neighbor resizing and preserved transparency. The previous portrait and token are in `../superseded/men-at-arms-before-v4/`.
