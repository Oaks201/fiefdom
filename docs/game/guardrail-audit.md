# Guardrail audit (T17)

One row per Chapter 16 guardrail: where it lives in the code, the test that proves it, and where it shows on screen. Every test named here passes in `npm test` (see the T17 hand-off for the run). Screenshots are in `docs/game/screens/`.

**Brought up to date on 2026-11-11 (rules 3.14.0), before Patch 10,** for what Patches 6 to 9 changed on the health side: the catch-up figures (D-46), the rest day (D-54), the rulers' dares (D-66), and Remedy's terms, requests and Audience, Faithful at the Scale, the plateau siege, the Heart Oak and Vigor (D-69 to D-77). Two tests were added for it: the Goblin's food dare against the floor (`rivals.test.ts`) and Remedy asking nothing while the player is away (`healerRequests.test.ts`). Five test names the T17 rows cited had been renamed since, two because a Respite day no longer moves a contract's end (D-54); they now read as the tests do. Patch 7 (reach, the rivals taking land, Dominion marks) changed nothing on the health side.

## Notes for the owner

- **Fitbit tokens off Windows.** `healthIpc.ts` encrypts tokens with `safeStorage` when the OS offers it and otherwise stores them in plain text beside the ledger. Windows (DPAPI) always offers it, so the release this audit covers is fine; a macOS or Linux build without a keychain would not be.
- **Medical supervision turns Remedy's terms into advice.** With it confirmed, her calorie limit and step pool no longer become the Charter's, and a limit below the floor stays the player's own (D-72); the catch-up figure still never goes under the floor.
- **Remedy's lines, read against this audit.** All 49 are approved (D-74, D-78), and her stage 2b and 3 lines were read against the "Crash dieting", "Losing weight too fast" and "Shame" rows when they were approved: they back them ("Eat to your limit, not under it.", "Do not chase it faster.", "Nothing is taken from you for it."). Two rest lines ask the player to spend a rest day "when you need it, not when you want it" and "when you are worn, not when you are bored"; that is her voice, but a rest day is earned and paid (D-54), so watch that it doesn't make one feel like a lapse. Any new Healer line keeps `approved: false` until Rich reads it.
- **A3 must keep the "Shame" row true.** The lines above are placeholders; the written text replaces them, and the Healer's lines show only once marked approved (`text.test.ts`). Reading the final text against this row is part of A3.
