Generated by `npm run sim -- --targets` (`sim/targets.ts`); do not edit by hand. 740 campaigns: Perfect, Steadfast, Committed and Wavering with the greedy player (40 seeds each; Steadfast to 100 seeds for G-08 alone, C-72), Steadfast steered along one path (each rival), two (Orc and Goblin; Dwarf and Archmage), three, and all four (70-week horizon); and the smarter player for every profile, and steered along each of those mixes (90-week horizon; Patch 5, P5-01) (845 s). The targets are `docs/redesign/blueprint.md` section 7; "Steadfast" is its greedy player, and the focused player follows one rival's path.
