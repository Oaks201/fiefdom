# Painted tavern assets

Created with the built-in image generation tool for Fiefdom. The final images are bundled locally by Vite and work offline.

- `src/renderer/src/assets/art/tavern-desk.png`: full desk scenery, including candle, coins, letter, quill and inkwell. `DeskProps` displays it behind the existing UI, keeping the props at the viewport edges.
- `src/renderer/src/assets/art/painted-parchment.png`: light paper material used by page sheets, dialogs and contract documents.
- Wax seals remain resolution-independent SVGs, with thicker wax rims, recessed faces and embossed marks.

## Desk generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production static background artwork for a medieval habit-ledger desktop app. Primary request: an inviting hand-painted fantasy tavern writing desk, cartoony medieval style inspired by the chunky sculpted materials and warm painted shading of Hearthstone and World of Warcraft. Landscape 16:9 composition, ideally 2560x1440. Camera looks straight down, perfectly flat overhead tabletop, no horizon, no perspective room. The entire middle 80% of the canvas is EMPTY dark golden-brown oak planks with broad curved wood grain, chunky beveled plank edges, scattered small nicks, warm amber illumination, restrained painterly texture. Important: the app will put a huge rectangular parchment UI over the center, so decorative objects must sit ONLY at the extreme left and right margins and bottom corners, leaving the top 8% calm dark wood too. Along the very far left: part of a sturdy antique brass candleholder with a short glowing candle near upper left, a few thick gold coins near lower left. Along the very far right: a cream feather quill and a squat blue-black ink bottle with a brass rim near lower right, a small folded blank letter partially cropped at upper right. Optional subtle carved timber corner details. Rich amber highlights, walnut shadows, quiet dark teal accents, exaggerated friendly chunky forms, polished painted game environment art, crisp wood carving, clean readable broad forms. Keep the desk bright enough to appreciate but darker than parchment. NO central paper, NO ledger, NO books in the center, NO text, NO letters, NO logos, NO UI elements, NO characters, NO photorealism. Fill full canvas edge to edge.

## Parchment generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production flat parchment material for a medieval fantasy ledger UI, used as a CSS background behind readable dark text. Primary request: beautifully hand-painted warm ivory parchment texture in the cartoony medieval fantasy style of Hearthstone / World of Warcraft game UI materials. Landscape rectangular material texture, ideally 1536x1024. Straight overhead orthographic view. The image is ALL PAPER, edge to edge; no surrounding surface, no objects, no cast shadow, no transparent exterior. Subtle soft butter-cream center, honey-gold softly weathered margins, broad gentle painterly variations, sparse tiny fibres and faint wear, barely visible natural creases, pleasantly thick tactile vellum. Clean central 85% must be even light cream with very low contrast and no distracting stains for small dark text. Only the outermost 5% may have slightly stronger aged patina. No drawings, no writing, no symbols, no lettering, no text, no ruled lines, no borders, no ornate scrollwork, no burn marks, no holes. Not gritty or photographic. A finished premium game UI texture, understated and luminous.
