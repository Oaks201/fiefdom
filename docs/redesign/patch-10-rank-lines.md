# Patch 10 stage 4: the rulers’ words on a rank (for Rich’s approval)

Drafted 2026-11-18 by Claude (L2, D-93). **Approved by Rich as written (D-94)** and moved into `rivalLines.json` under each ruler’s `ranks` on 2026-11-20, so every build speaks them.

**Where they show.** When your Dominion on a ruler’s path reaches a new rank, a big-moment card opens at the day’s close: his portrait, “Ugrak’s path: rank III”, what the path gives you now, and his line beneath. Each rank has its own whole line, so these are approved one at a time, not as parts.

**To approve:** move a line from `rivalLines.draft.json` into `rivalLines.json` under the same ruler, in a `ranks` entry keyed by the rank (`"1"` to `"5"`), editing it as you like. A line left out shows nothing.

**What each rank gives** (so the lines can nod to it): every rank adds a step to the path’s perk; ranks III and V add their own, and the Archmage’s rank I foretells tomorrow’s surge. A line that names a perk would need rewording if a perk moves to another rank.
